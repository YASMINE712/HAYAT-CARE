Timezone and reminder details

Each program follows its saved timezone. When clocks move forward, a nonexistent time shifts forward by the clock change. A repeated clock time generates one dose. If you travel, review the program with your clinician before changing its timezone. Snooze delays the alert by ten minutes, not the prescribed schedule or confirmation window.
