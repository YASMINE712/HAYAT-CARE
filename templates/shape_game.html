This game tests your visual memory by having you remember sequences of colored shapes.

You'll be shown shapes briefly, then asked to recall them in order.

How to Play:

  1. Memorize the sequence of shapes shown
  2. After they disappear, recreate the sequence in the same order
  3. Get to higher levels by remembering longer sequences

Note: This is not a medical diagnosis tool, but can help identify potential memory issues.
